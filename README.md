# 1959 Ford F600 Build

Jacob's personal build. Cab and frame only are kept from the '59. Everything else is new or swapped.

- **Engine:** 99-03 Super Duty 7.3 Powerstroke, full custom rebuild
- **Target:** 400-450 hp AND bulletproof. Reliability first.
- **Transmission:** built 4x4 4R100 (soft-locked, open to revisiting)
- **Drivetrain:** 4x4 conversion on the ORIGINAL F600 frame
- **Bed:** custom welded flatbed with camper receiver built in
- **Use:** show piece that daily drives and tows a camper
- **Budget/timeline:** no deadline, budget flexible, buy right once

Separate from the 2011 F350 6.7 build and the El Coyote twin-turbo build. Don't cross parts or goals.

## Files

- `BUILD.md` - the full plan: decisions, game plan, shop work order, parts catalog, open items
- `notes/` - dated logs of conversations and decisions as they happen
- `reference/` - saved source material: 73psd factory specs, breakout images, photos from teardown, and `manuals/` (Ford workshop manual, service training books, torque specs, HPOP and fuel system sheets)

Everything on this truck gets saved here.
