- Jacob asked how to remove the rear cover. Advice: leave it on and mount the engine stand to it (the trans bolts to it, it carries the load). Pull it with the oil pan later: pan bolts thread into the cover's bottom flange, then perimeter bolts, two dowels, RTV'd to the block, pry at the tabs, slides back over the crank flange. Goes to Family Machine with the block for bake/blast.
