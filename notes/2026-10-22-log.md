- Oil pan bolts out, pan stuck on RTV. Told him: oil pan seal cutter is the tool; otherwise heat gun on the flange, thin putty knife tapped in flat, wedges once a gap opens, fishing line to saw the rest. Air hammer with a scraper bit works if the pan is a throwaway. Protect the block rail and rear cover flange, don't pry against them. Front and rear corners are the thick spots. Inspect pan for pitting, replace if rusted inside.
- Jacob asked if good lifters need to stay in order. First answer said flat-tappet, keep in order. CORRECTED same day: the 7.3 Powerstroke uses hydraulic ROLLER lifters (every vendor page lists them as roller). Order is nice-to-have, not critical, and new roller lifters on a good used cam is fine. Still bag them by cylinder and I/E.
- Same for rocker arms: keep each rocker with its own fulcrum and pushrod, in position (zip-tie the set, tag 1I/1E etc). Arm and fulcrum wear-mate, pushrod cup wears to its ball. Pedestal bolts stay OEM, one bag. New valves reset the tip wear pattern, so the shop judges rockers on fulcrum galling and tip pitting and reuses good sets.
- Jacob: "maybe I should go new just to be safe." Rec: new lifters (KDD Gen 2 $395 or PSP $214), new chromoly pushrods ($270-325), reuse rockers after shop inspection, cam decision waits for the shop (stock vs Colt Stage 2 $749). Prices from vendor pages 2026-10-22, recorded in BUILD.md game plan.
