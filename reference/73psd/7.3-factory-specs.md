# 7.3L Power Stroke Factory Specs

Source: https://73psd.com/factory-specs/engine/ (saved 2026-10-20, flagged by Jacob). Images `98_7.3L.png` and `99_7.3L.png` are the engine breakouts from the same page (up-to-1998 and 1999+). The page also links a 1999 F-Super Duty 250-550 Powertrain Engine Workshop Manual PDF, but that link was dead (404) on 2026-10-20.

## Where the engine serial number is

On the rear oil cooler where the oil filter assembly mounts to the block. 1999+ engines also have a serial number label on the driver-side valve cover.

## Forged vs powdered-metal rods, by engine serial number

Navistar started a test run of powdered-metal rods (PMR) in 2001. 14,965 sets went in alongside forged rods before they switched to PMR for the rest of production.

| Engine S/N range | Rods |
|---|---|
| Start of production to 1425746 | Forged |
| 1425747 to 1440712 | PMR |
| 1440713 to 1498318 | Forged |
| 1498319 to end of production | PMR |

Why it matters: forged rods take up to about 600 rwhp and tend to bend instead of break. PMR rods are rated about 500 rwhp or less and tend to snap.

For this build it is a curiosity, not a decision: new Industrial Injection forged rods are going in either way. Still worth recording what came out.

## Notes for the build

- Stock bore 4.11 is the baseline for the oversize decision. II coated pistons: std / .020 / .040 only.
- Stock CR is 17.5:1; the II coated piston is 17.3:1.
- Weight: 920 lb dry. Matters for F600 engine mounts, crossmember, and front spring rate.
- Oil: 15 qt. Size the oil cooler and plan the LPOP/HPOP refresh around the two-stage system.
